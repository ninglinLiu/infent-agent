Vendored math.ts, species.ts, world.ts and renderer.ts. The renderer is adapted
for white water and lavender ink, finer cells, lighter fish/wakes, and no grid
or solid body tiles. ocean.ts is an omniAI host with a 24fps cap, slower time,
42 fish, two sharks, pause, reduced motion, offscreen and hidden-tab suspension.
No additional packages, CDN requests, model calls or market-data coupling.
