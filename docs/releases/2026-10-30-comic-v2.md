# Four research decisions v2

The four-scene comic retains its existing black and white artwork and animated teaching candles. New attempts use an append-only v2 script with realistic research trade-offs, feedback after each saved choice and a fourth decision that atomically completes the attempt. No answer is graded by hindsight profit.

Scene 1 compares near-term operating evidence with a longer-term valuation framework. Scene 2 offers a 40-share allocation or cash. Scene 3 adapts to the actual holding: retain or exit existing shares; keep cash or enter at 79.50. Scene 4 retains the allocation or sells remaining shares at 194. All sixteen combinations complete, with four persisted choice events, completion timestamp and optional learner reasoning. Four economic paths end at 13,760, 9,180, 10,000 or 14,580 simulated credits. Realised and unrealised P&L are separate; there are no dividends, fees, taxes or cash interest.

Original v1 content, attempts and choices remain unchanged. Existing users can open the new exercise explicitly while retaining their earlier records. Reset creates another attempt, preserving completed history. The database derives balances and owner identity; clients cannot set either. The public invoker dispatches by script version to private transaction functions. Operation IDs and revisions protect against duplicate submissions and stale updates. The legacy helper is revoked from browser roles and called only by the checked dispatcher.
